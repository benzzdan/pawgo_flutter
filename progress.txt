## Codebase Patterns
- Chat screen (`walker_chat_screen.dart`) handles both owner and walker roles — no separate screens needed
- Quick actions (status updates) are walker-only via `RoleService.instance.activeRole.value == 'walker'` check
- Active walk screen: use `_isWalker ? _ownerName : _walkerName` for correct other-party in chat
- Edge Functions: use `supabase/edge-runtime` with main router at `functions/main/index.ts`
- Kong WebSocket: needs dedicated route with `key_in_query: true` for Realtime WS connections
- No direct DB inserts/updates for bookings — all go through Edge Functions
